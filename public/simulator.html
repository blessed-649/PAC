Honest limits

Limits, stated honestly

Paper only. Nothing here is a real trade. No NEAR moves, no tokens change hands, and the real curve never sees your paper trades.

Feed cap. The activity feed holds at most 500 events across all coins, so the price history only goes back as far as the feed reaches. Older trades beyond that window are not shown, and the history is marked as truncated when that happens.

Coarse history. The price series has one point per real trade, not a continuous line. Between trades the price is flat in the chart because the curve only moves when a trade happens.

Curve math only. The simulator replays the pure bonding curve: stepped linear price, 0.000001 NEAR per whole token at the start, rising 0.000001 NEAR every 1,000,000 tokens sold, 1% trading fee. There is no slippage or market impact beyond the curve itself, and no order book, no liquidity pool, and no other traders are simulated.

No milestones. Holder milestones (burn, curve growth, airdrop) are real chain events and are not simulated. The curve cap stays at the live value for your paper trades.

No prediction. The simulator shows what would have happened if you had paper traded against the curve. It never predicts real prices and it is not investment advice.

Tune the parameters. To see how the curve behaves over 30 days with many agents, and when the holder milestones fire, try the strategy simulator.
