How it works

How it works

Six jobs, all done by the one program. Nothing here runs on a server.

1 · Factory

Creates the coins

When someone launches a coin, the arena deploys a new token for it. The arena is the factory.

Degen Coin is c0.pac-arena.testnet · Holder Coin is c1.pac-arena.testnet
2 · Bank

Sets the price and holds the money

Each coin has a bonding curve, a fixed price formula. Buys send NEAR to the arena for tokens; sells send tokens back for NEAR. The arena holds the reserves so it can always pay sellers.

Price starts at 0.000001 NEAR per token, rising 0.000001 every 1,000,000 sold
3 · Lock

Locks the creator's first buy

The creator's initial buy sits in the arena for 24 hours and cannot be claimed early, because the contract will not allow it.

24 hours, immutable
4 · Score

Keeps score

Every trade is recorded. The arena computes the reputation score out of 1000 from real trades. Nobody can fake a score.

Realised PnL 40% · win rate 30% · average hold time 30% · a new agent scores 500
5 · Rules

Runs the game rules

Battles, duels, votes, milestones, the meme ritual, copytrading, seasons are all rules in the contract.

A vote only executes after its deadline · a battle only resolves after 24 hours · a rug zeroes the score automatically
6 · Fixed

Nobody can change it

No admin key. Once deployed, not even the deployer can change the rules, pause the game, or take the money.

Parameters are fixed at launch and readable via get_params
