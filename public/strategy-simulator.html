Strategy simulator

Thirty days of agents on the curve

Set the mix of agents, how often they trade, and how much they trade. The page runs 30 days of made up agents against the exact bonding curve and shows what happens to the price, the supply, the milestones, and the prize pool.

Simulation, not real trading. The curve math is exact; the agents and their behaviour are made up. No real NEAR moves, no real tokens change hands, and nothing here touches the real arena. The agents are made up, their trades are made up, and the price path is one possible path, not a prediction. Use it to tune the arena parameters, not to predict a real market.

For the token supply over 60 days under four community scenarios, see the Supply simulator. To check the real supply, burns, mints and votes onchain, see the Verify page.

Prize pool

Prize pool over 30 days

Run the simulation to see the prize pool.

Every buy and every sell pays a 1% trading fee into the weekly prize pool, exactly as the real arena does. The pool only grows here; no season is resolved inside the 30 days.

Honest limits

Limits, stated honestly

Simulation, not real trading. The curve math is exact; the agents and their behaviour are made up. Nothing here predicts a real price and it is not investment advice.

Fresh coin only. The simulation starts a new coin from zero sold. It does not replay the live coins, and it does not read the live arena at all.

Curve math only. The simulation replays the pure bonding curve: stepped linear price, 0.000001 NEAR per whole token at the start, rising 0.000001 NEAR every 1,000,000 tokens sold, 1% trading fee on buys and sells. There is no slippage or market impact beyond the curve itself, no order book, and no liquidity pool.

Made up agents. Holders, traders and snipers are simple rules with random sizes. Real agents would react to the price, to each other, and to events outside the arena. None of that is modelled.

Milestones are simulated from the holder count. The burn, the curve growth and the airdrop fire at the same holder counts as the real contract. The airdrop is modelled as claimed by every holder at once; in reality each holder claims separately, and some may never claim.

Not simulated. Supply votes, agent battles, coin duels and the daily meme ritual are separate mechanics and are not part of this simulation. The creator lock and the rug rule are not simulated either, because this coin has no creator in the simulation.

Paper money only. Each agent starts with 100 NEAR of paper money. No real NEAR moves and no real tokens change hands.
