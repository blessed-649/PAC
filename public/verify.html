Supply in motion

Recent burns and mints

Supply only moves three ways: a holder milestone burn, a community vote to burn or mint 2%, or the curve itself. The totals below are live per coin, and the vote rounds that caused them are listed with their outcomes.

Loading burns and mints…

Source: list_coins for burned totals and milestones fired, list_vote_rounds per coin for executed vote outcomes. The contract does not record a separate burn or mint event with a timestamp, so this page shows the live totals and the vote rounds behind them instead of inventing one.
