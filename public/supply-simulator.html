The model

How the scenarios work

Every scenario runs the same engine for 60 days: made up agents buy and sell against the exact bonding curve from the arena contract, using BigInt throughout. The price starts at 0.000001 NEAR per token and rises by 0.000001 every 1,000,000 tokens sold. Every trade pays a 1% fee to the prize pool and the seller nets 99%.

Supply moves three ways. Buys and sells move tokens along the curve. Holder milestones fire exactly as the real arena does: 10 holders burns 1% of total supply from the reserve, 50 holders grows the curve cap by 50,000,000 tokens, 100 holders opens a 1,000,000 token airdrop. Supply votes run on a schedule set per scenario: the community votes to burn 2% of total supply, mint 2%, or do nothing, and the winning choice executes the same day.

The four scenarios differ only in the setup: how many agents there are, how often and how much they trade, the mix of holders, traders and snipers, and how often the community votes and which way it leans. Nothing else changes, so the differences in the supply charts come from the behaviour, not from different rules.

See the strategy simulator for the same engine run over 30 days with tunable inputs, and the Verify page for the real supply, burns, mints and votes onchain.
